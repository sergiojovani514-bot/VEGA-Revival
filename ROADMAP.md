# Roadmap

## v0.1 Compute Foundation

- Minimal Vulkan compute smoke test on Radeon VII (1002:66AF).
- Publish historical, explicitly scoped research results after provenance review.
- Collect reproducibility reports and independently test compatibility.
- Keep stress tests and unaudited harnesses out of the release.

## Research directions

- VEGA Compute: reviewed compute kernels.
- VEGA AI: experimental PyTorch and local inference work.
- VEGA Memory: HBM2, LDS, and cache research.
- VEGA Geometry: compute geometry and alternative geometry pipelines.
- VEGA Gaming: research into upscaling and frame generation.
- VEGA Research: GCN architecture study.

These are plans, not shipped capabilities. Hardware or driver support will be claimed only after reproducible testing.
