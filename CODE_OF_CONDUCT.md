# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in VEGA Revival welcoming and respectful, regardless of experience, hardware, operating system, or background.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Share reproducible evidence and distinguish measurements from hypotheses.
- Accept good-faith corrections and acknowledge uncertainty.
- Protect personal data and respect third-party licenses.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, doxxing, threats, discriminatory language, and knowingly unsafe instructions are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation for conduct that violates this policy. Contact the maintainers privately through the repository's GitHub contact route to report an incident. This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant 2.1; final provenance and licensing review is required before publication.
